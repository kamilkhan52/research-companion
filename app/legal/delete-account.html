This page explains how to delete your {{APP_NAME}} account and everything attached to it. You do not need the app installed to read it or to make the request.

Deleting your account is permanent. Your papers, discussions, notes and their history are erased, and we cannot restore them afterwards. If you want to keep any of it, save it before you start.

In the app — the fastest way

  1. Open {{APP_NAME}} and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Sign in again when asked. This is deliberate: a recent sign-in proves it is really you, and a stored session is not enough.
  4. Type the confirmation exactly as shown, and confirm.

Deletion starts immediately and usually finishes within about ten minutes — it deliberately waits out a safety window first, so that a deployment happening at the same moment cannot leave half of it done. It continues on our server even if you close the app, and it resumes by itself if something fails partway. You will be signed out, and the app will show that the account was deleted.

By email — if you cannot reach the app

Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the subject Delete my account, from the email address the account uses. That is how we confirm the request is yours, so a message from any other address cannot be actioned.

We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, and in practice much sooner. We will reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Every project you own, with its full history — reading lists, retrieved and uploaded paper text, discussion transcripts, notes and syntheses, figures, covers and caches.
  • Projects you had previously deleted and that were still recoverable.
  • Your settings: model, voice, speech and reading preferences.
  • Narrated audio generated for you.
  • Any reports you filed about AI-generated content, including the notes you wrote on them.
  • Your account record, and finally your sign-in identity itself, so the account can no longer be used to sign in.
  • On your device: your session, cached images and audio, and local app state — cleared when the deletion is confirmed.

Work in progress is handled too. Anything still running for your account is stopped before the data it touches is removed, so nothing gets written back after deletion.

What is kept, and why

Two records survive, and neither contains your reading, your notes, or your email address:

  • A pseudonymous deletion record — that a deletion happened, and when. It holds a one-way identifier rather than your account identifier. It exists so a repeated or interrupted request cannot resurrect an account we have already erased.
  • The bare identifiers of projects that once existed — the name of the slot, not its contents. Reusing an identifier would let an old authorisation reach a new project, so we retire identifiers permanently instead of freeing them.

Beyond our own systems: operational logs held by our hosting provider expire on that provider's schedule, and content already sent to other providers is subject to their retention. For the models that generate discussion we restrict routing to providers that do not retain it; for narration and web search we have no such guarantee. None of it can be recalled by us. The Privacy Policy sets out which is which.

Deleting less than everything

You do not have to delete your account to get rid of a piece of work. Deleting a single project in the app removes it from your library and keeps a recoverable copy so a mistake can be undone. That copy has no automatic expiry today — it stays until you delete your account, which erases it.
